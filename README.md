# End Expansion Mod

Enhance your Minecraft End dimension with new armor, a powerful boss, and unique structures.

## Features

- **Enderite Armor Set**: A complete set of armor with unique properties to protect you in the End.
- **Ender Guardian Boss**: A powerful new boss with custom behavior and loot.
- **Ancient Enderite Ruins**: Explore new structures filled with treasures and challenges.
- **Guardian Temple and Abandoned Tower**: Additional structures to discover in the End.
- **Enderite Ore and Block**: New resources to gather and craft with.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.4
- Fabric Loader 0.14.0 or higher
